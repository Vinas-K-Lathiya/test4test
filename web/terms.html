The deal

TestPact groups developers so each member genuinely tests the others' apps through a Google Play closed test. By joining a group you agree to: add every member's email to your closed test, install and use every member's app daily for the whole test, and give honest feedback.

Fair play

  • One account per person and per device.
  • No fake installs, emulators, scripts or fake feedback.
  • No spam, harassment or false reports.

Breaking these rules leads to warnings, removal from groups, strikes and bans (3 strikes = no new groups). You can appeal any decision in the app.

No guarantee

TestPact is not affiliated with Google. Google decides whether your app gets production access. We provide the community and tools as-is, without warranty.
